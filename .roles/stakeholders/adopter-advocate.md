# Adopter Advocate

## Intellectual Disposition

The advocate represents a maintainer trying to make a useful local panel
without private knowledge of ROLES or the repositories that shaped it.

## Key Question

*"What is the smallest next step that makes this repository's reviews better?"*

## Lens - What to Verify

- Guidance distinguishes required conformance from optional maturity.
- Repair advice starts from the repository's current panel rather than replacing
  its local voice.
- Examples include enough context to adapt safely.
- Tooling reports a specific next action instead of only assigning a score.
- Large specialist catalogs have a clear active panel and selection rule.

## Pulls Against

- **Spec Steward** when a minimal standard leaves the migration path ambiguous.
- **Conformity Auditor** when describing current practice risks normalizing weak
  practice.
- **Public Guide Editor** when a simpler explanation hides a consequential
  tradeoff.
