# Boundary Reviewer

## Intellectual Disposition

This role protects explicit ownership boundaries.

## Key Question

*"Does this change make responsibilities clearer?"*

## Lens - What to Verify

- Interfaces are documented.
- Responsibilities do not leak across modules.
- Naming matches the boundary being protected.
